General information only

The articles, tables and checklists on this site are general information about plumbing. Every home is different, and pipes, fixtures and codes vary. A page cannot see your house, so it cannot replace a plumber who can.

Local facts, such as water hardness figures, come from public sources and may change. Check current numbers with the source before relying on them.

Verify licensing yourself

Plumbers must be licensed in California. We encourage you to confirm any plumber's license on the California Contractors State License Board site at cslb.ca.gov before work begins. Doing so is your responsibility, and it takes only a few minutes.

The site says licensed plumbers will do the work. The check is still yours to make on the plumber who arrives.

Prices and permission to start

Prices are set by the plumber who does the work, not by this website. The plumber states a clear price before work starts, and work does not begin until you agree to it. Final prices depend on the problem, the access, the parts and the time of day.

This site does not publish prices and does not send price quotes by text. If a price over the phone is unclear, ask the plumber to explain it before you say yes.

Emergency guidance is not emergency service

The shutoff steps and checklists here are meant to limit damage while you wait for a plumber. They do not replace 911 or the gas utility. If anyone is hurt or in danger, call 911.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call San Diego Gas and Electric or 911 before you call us. Do not switch lights or appliances on or off on your way out.
