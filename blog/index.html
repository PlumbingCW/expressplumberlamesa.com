What the blog is for

This blog answers the questions that come before a phone call. How do I tell a leak from condensation? Should I flush the water heater? What does a hydro jet actually do? Each post gives a quick answer first, then the steps, the limits of a do-it-yourself fix and the point where a plumber is the right call.

Posts are written by the La Mesa Express Plumbing team as a group, in organization voice. They avoid prices, because real prices depend on your pipes and your house. They name cost factors instead, so you know what to ask.
