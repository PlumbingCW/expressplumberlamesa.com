The area

Where Northmont is

Northmont is a residential area of northern La Mesa. The sources we found tie the name to Northmont Park at 6030 Severin Drive and to Northmont Elementary School, and both have La Mesa addresses in ZIP 91942.

We did not find an official boundary map. So we do not claim exact borders. We describe the area by anchor streets and landmarks, and we ask for your street on the call.

To the north, the Lake Murray reservoir belongs to the City of San Diego, not La Mesa. Wikipedia notes that La Mesa's northernmost neighborhoods use the lake for recreation.

Reference points

Landmarks around Northmont

These real places help us place a Northmont address.

Northmont Park

The park covers 5.05 acres on Severin Drive. The City acquired the land in 1967 and finished the current layout in 1988. A small parking lot is reached from Severin Drive near Amaya Drive.

Severin Grossmont Hills

A neighborhood group uses this name for the area near Severin Drive and Murray Drive, at the western entrance to La Mesa. East County Magazine reported in 2022 that La Mesa Public Works approved a small pedestrian park and historical marker there.

Original developers

The same article names Earl Severin and Urban DeCamp as the original developers and says Dr. Charles M. Johnson bought the land in 1865.

Grossmont next door

Grossmont Center and its trolley stop lie to the east. See our Grossmont page if your street is closer to that side.

Home plumbing

Common household problems and where to read more

Most home calls start with one of these. Each links to a page with the details.

Slow or backed-up drains

A slow sink or tub often starts in the trap or branch line. See drain cleaning for what a plumber does about it.

Toilet trouble

A running, weak-flushing or rocking toilet usually has a simple cause. See toilet repair.

No hot water

A cold shower can mean a failed element, thermostat or burner. See water heater repair. California Health and Safety Code section 19211 also requires bracing against earthquake movement.

Sewage in the lowest drain

If sewage rises in the lowest tub or floor drain, the blockage is probably past your fixtures. See sewer line repair.

Urgent

Water where it should not be? Do this first

Waiting for a plumber feels long when water is moving. These steps limit the damage and keep everyone safe.

  • Find the main shutoff valve and turn it clockwise until it stops.
  • For one leaking fixture, close the small stop valve on its supply line under the sink or behind the toilet.
  • If a drain is backing up, stop using sinks, tubs, washers and dishwashers.
  • Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the wet area, and keep the power off near standing water.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call San Diego Gas and Electric or 911 before you call a plumber.

Water

Hard water in Northmont

Water in La Mesa comes from the Helix Water District. Helix reported average hardness of 206 mg/L, or 12.0 grains per gallon, for 2025, with a range of 155 to 250 mg/L. On the common USGS scale that counts as very hard. Blends can differ by street, so check your own tap or ask Helix.

Hard water leaves white scale on faucets and shower heads and can build up inside a water heater tank. If that sounds familiar, water softener and filtration options are worth a look.
