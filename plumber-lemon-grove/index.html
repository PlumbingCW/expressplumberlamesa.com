Water

What Helix water does inside Lemon Grove plumbing

Helix Water District serves Lemon Grove, and its 2025 report listed average hardness at 12.0 grains per gallon. That is very hard on the USGS scale, so scale collects in tank heaters, faucet aerators and shower valves. Blends can vary by street, so check your own tap or ask Helix about your address.

For renters, that often shows up as a faucet that sprays sideways or a shower with a weak stream. A quick soak of the aerator in vinegar can help, and persistent trouble calls for faucet repair. Owners with older tank heaters should ask about a flush and anode check before the tank fails.

Reporting

A simple written trail for tenants

  1. Stop the water

    Close the fixture stop or the unit valve. Note the time and what you turned off.

  2. Photograph and date

    Take wide and close shots of the wet area. A short video of a dripping ceiling is even better.

  3. Tell the landlord in writing

    Send a text or email to the manager listing the problem, the location and what you did. Keep the reply.

  4. Call us if it cannot wait

    If water is still spreading, call (619) 927-0312 and tell us you are a tenant. We will ask for the landlord's contact to plan the repair.

Before we arrive

Before we arrive at your Lemon Grove address

Whether you rent a duplex or own a bungalow, a few minutes of prep makes the visit cleaner.

Shut the right valve

In a duplex or small complex, the unit valve may sit under a sink or outside by the meter. If you cannot find it, say so on the phone.

Power down wet areas

Switch off breakers for laundry nooks and kitchens where water has reached outlets. Do not use extension cords across wet floors.

Open a path

Clear the hall to the bathroom or the closet holding the heater. Move small items off the floor so a mop and tools can fit.

For owners

Landlord checklist before a small leak becomes a claim

A slow leak under a rental sink is easy to miss between inspections. Check supply lines and shutoff valves at turnover, and replace any that are crusty or green. A cheap part replaced early beats water damage in the unit below.

If a tenant reports repeated clogs, ask for a camera look instead of another snake. A sewer inspection will show whether roots or a bellied pipe are the cause. The result gives you something concrete to approve.
