How to choose the right service

Urgent or planned?

Urgent

Water is moving, sewage is surfacing or heat is gone. Call right away, whatever the hour, and do the shutoff steps first.

Same day

The problem is stable but will get worse, such as a slow drain or a dripping valve. Ask for a same-day appointment.

Planned

You are replacing a fixture, adding a line or upgrading a water heater. Call during the day and ask for a clear price.

Hard water and plumbing choices

Helix Water District reported very hard water for 2025, with an average of 12.0 grains per gallon. Scale can shorten the life of tank heaters and clog aerators, so ask the plumber about flushing and descaling during any visit.

If scale keeps coming back, look at softener and filter options before replacing parts again.
