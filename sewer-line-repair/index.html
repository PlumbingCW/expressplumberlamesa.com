Repair, not cleaning

When the sewer pipe itself has failed

Sewer line repair means fixing or replacing the pipe that carries waste from your house to the street after a crack, offset joint, root break or collapse has damaged it. If sewage is backing into fixtures in La Mesa, CA, our 24/7 emergency line at (619) 927-0312 sends a licensed plumber. Stop using water in the house while you wait.

Cleaning and repair are different jobs. Cleaning removes what is inside the pipe, and repair fixes the pipe. If a line clogs again and again, a cable or jet cleaning of the main line only buys time while the damage stays.

A camera gives the answer. A recorded camera pass down the line shows the break, how far it sits from the cleanout and how much of the pipe is affected. With that, the choice between spot repair, lining and replacement rests on facts.

Warning signs

Signs the pipe is broken and not just clogged

We see these patterns in La Mesa and in neighboring Lemon Grove. Any one of them is worth a camera look.

Backups that keep returning

Cleaning helps for a few weeks, then the same fixtures fail. That pattern often means a root entry or a sag.

A cable that hits a hard stop

A stop at the same distance every time suggests a break, an offset joint or a collapsed section.

Wet or sunken ground along the line

Wastewater leaking from a crack saturates the ground and can lower it above the pipe.

Sewer smell in the yard

Odor outside, away from any vent, points to a leak in the buried line.

Slow drains with no clog found

If a cable finds nothing yet flow is poor, the pipe may be bellied or partly collapsed.

Urgent

Sewer backup in the house right now

Sewage coming up through a tub, shower or floor drain is a health hazard, and our line stays open all night for it. Repairs that can wait until morning are scheduled as same-day appointments when available. Do these steps first.

  • Stop all water use, including the washer, dishwasher and showers.
  • Keep everyone out of the affected room, pets included.
  • Avoid touching standing sewage with bare skin and wear rubber gloves and boots if you must go in.
  • Turn off power to the area only if you can reach the panel without stepping in water.
  • Lift rugs and belongings off the floor and open windows for airflow.
  • Take photos for your insurer before any cleanup begins.

Safety steps

What to do while a sewer line problem waits for repair

Cut water use

Stop using toilets, sinks and washers that drain toward the damaged line. Fewer flushes means less wastewater in the house.

Mark the wet spot

If sewage surfaces in a yard, keep people and pets off it and tell the dispatcher where it is.

Call 811 before digging

If you plan any digging yourself, call 811 first so underground lines are marked. A plumber will arrange this for a dig repair.

Do not enter an open trench

Excavations can cave in without warning. Keep everyone back from the edge.

Backups

Why a sewer line backs up and what we do about it

A backup happens when the line cannot carry flow to the street. The usual causes are a blockage of grease or paper, tree roots in a joint, a belly where waste settles, or a break that lets soil fall into the pipe. The lowest drain in the house shows it first.

Our first goal is to stop the water in your home, usually by cabling the line from the cleanout. Once flow returns, we run a camera to learn why it happened. Skip that step and you may be back in the same spot in a month.

Then you choose. A clean, undamaged line needs only maintenance. A damaged one needs a repair plan, and we explain the options in plain terms with a clear price first. Wait until you have seen the video before agreeing to dig.

Older homes

Failing cast iron pipe: scale, cracks and slow rot

Older homes often have cast iron drain and sewer pipe. It lasts for decades, but it corrodes from the inside and sometimes from the outside. Rust scale builds up in layers and narrows the opening, so a cast iron line can clog even when nothing foreign is in it.

On camera we look for flaking interior walls, cracks along the bottom of the pipe and joints that have shifted. A cast iron line can be sound in one stretch and failing five feet away, so we scope the length before recommending anything.

Repair choices are a short section cut out and replaced with modern pipe, a liner if the shape holds, or full replacement if much of the pipe is thin. If your home is old enough to have cast iron, ask for the video before deciding.

Two ways to fix it

Trenchless pipe lining or dig and replace: how they compare

Both methods end with a working pipe. They differ in what gets torn up and in which damage they can handle.

Lining cannot straighten a pipe that has collapsed or sagged. Sometimes the answer is a short dig for one bad section and lining for the rest.

Spot repair

A cracked line with roots: when a spot repair is enough

A localized crack or a joint with root intrusion does not always mean replacing the whole line. If the camera shows one damaged stretch and the rest is sound, a spot repair cuts out the bad piece and installs new pipe with couplings. Plastic sewer pipe such as PVC suits this well.

Roots are cut out first with a cable or a water jet used on sound pipe so the break is visible. We then expose the pipe at that spot, replace the section and backfill. A camera pass afterward shows the new joint is tight and the line is clear.

A spot repair fits when damage is limited to one or two places. If the camera shows cracks along most of the line, repeating spot repairs costs more than a single lining or replacement. We tell you which case you have.

Septic homes

Septic systems are a different trade

If your home is on a septic tank instead of a city sewer, say so on the first call. Tank pumping, drain field work and baffle repair belong to septic contractors, and we are plumbers. A backup in a septic home may come from a full tank, a clogged filter or a failed drain field, and each has a different fix.

What we can do is clear the house drain lines and repair the pipe between the house and the tank. If the problem turns out to be the tank itself, we will tell you so and you can call the right trade.

The job

From video to finished pipe

  2. Mark the spot

    A line locator shows the depth and position of the damage, which keeps digging to the minimum needed.

  3. Pick the method

    After the repair we run water and a final camera pass to confirm the joints are tight and the flow is clear.

What shapes the price of a sewer repair

Two sewer repairs rarely cost the same, because no two broken pipes are the same. You get a clear price before work starts.

Length of damaged pipe

One cracked joint is a small job. A long run with cracks along its length is a bigger one.

Depth and location

A pipe under a lawn is cheaper to reach than one under a driveway, patio or slab.

Method

Spot repair, lining and full replacement use different crews, materials and time.

Pipe material and size

Cast iron, clay and plastic are repaired differently, and larger diameters cost more.

Restoration afterward

Dug lawn, concrete or pavers must be put back, and that work belongs in the price.

Some homeowner policies cover sudden pipe failure and the cleanup after a backup, but often not gradual root or wear damage. Ask your insurer about both, and keep the camera video as evidence.
